# CGA — Built for You

Running a golf association by hand is a second job. Tracking scores on paper, calculating standings in a spreadsheet, texting out pairings, remembering who's paid dues — it adds up fast.

This site handles all of it.

---

## What it replaces

| What you used to do manually | What the site does automatically |
|---|---|
| Calculate POY points after every tournament | Computed instantly from scores — ties averaged, ineligible players handled correctly |
| Maintain standings in a spreadsheet | Live standings page, updated the moment results are entered |
| Figure out flight assignments by hand | Drag-and-drop admin panel — assign players to flights in minutes |
| Type up and share pairings | Pairings page always current, accessible from any phone |
| Keep track of who's made their points | Per-player +/− shown across every tournament |
| Answer "what are the rules?" questions | Rules page members can check themselves |

---

## How it works for the manager

1. After a tournament, open the **Admin panel** (password protected)
2. Drag players into their flights, enter their scores
3. Hit export — standings, POY points, and results are all updated

That's it. No spreadsheet. No math. No follow-up texts.

---

## What members get

- Check standings and POY points anytime from their phone
- See the full schedule and upcoming tournaments
- Look up pairings and tee times
- Review eligibility rules without asking

---

## The bottom line

You built this association. This site just takes the paperwork off your plate so you can focus on the golf.

It was built specifically for CGA — the flights, the POY algorithm, the Points to Make system — everything matches the way you already run things.
